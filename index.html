ABOUT

About me

I'm a software engineer with a front-end focus and three years of professional experience building web and cross-platform mobile applications. I work mainly in JavaScript and TypeScript with React, Angular and React Native, connect them to REST APIs and Node.js services, and care about products that feel fast and dependable, and code that stays clean as a product grows.

At Syftet Ltd., I improved the performance of React Native apps by optimising component rendering, preventing unnecessary re-renders and tightening application logic and data handling. I implemented an offline-first architecture with SQLite, so users could keep working with their data without an internet connection and have it synchronise once connectivity returned.

On larger front ends, I managed complex state and state transitions with Redux to keep data flow predictable across feature-rich applications, and built them from reusable, maintainable components shared across features. I've also delivered real-time features, using WebSockets in Angular for live messaging and updates between users.

Much of my work sits where teams meet. I collaborate closely with designers, backend engineers and project managers to turn designs into working features and integrate them with backend services, including alongside a Ruby on Rails team. A teammate at Syftet described me as “a proactive team player who is always willing to lend a hand.”

I'm now completing an MSc in Computer Science at University College Birmingham, where my dissertation measures gender and ethnicity bias in machine-learning CV screening, and I'm exploring AI-powered development: MCP servers, AI automation and agentic applications.
